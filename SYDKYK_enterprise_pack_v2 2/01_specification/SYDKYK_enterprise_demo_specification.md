# SYDKYK enterprise demo specification

Version 1.0. Prepared October 15, 2026 for SYDKYK product, sales engineering and implementation teams.

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. The supporting company and all policies and records are invented. Not legal advice. The deliverable is a build specification and supporting content pack; it does not modify, deploy or connect the existing application.

## 1 Purpose and recommended release
Build a guided enterprise demonstration that lets a prospect choose an employee-service use case, ask a grounded question, review a proposed action, see a human approval where needed, and inspect simulated execution evidence. Use a coherent 4,000-person Philippines company rather than unrelated canned examples. Ship the P0 scenarios first, with honest labels for catalog items that are specified but not implemented.
The demo should prove the distinction between answering from company knowledge, reading personal operational data, generating a draft, creating a mock transaction and handing a sensitive decision to a human. A fluent answer is not evidence that an integration ran. All action outcomes in the initial release are simulated.

## 4 Use-case scope and release priorities
The attached CSV and JSON catalog enumerates HR, IT, Finance and Workplace scenarios with stable IDs, prompts, source documents, modes, priorities and outcomes. All entries are marked specified_not_implemented. P0 is the recommended first demonstrable release; P1 expands grounded answers and routine transactions; P2 covers broader drafts and human handoffs.
Each UI entry must expose one of four modes: Grounded answer, Mock transaction, Draft and review, or Human handoff. A catalog entry must not become a clickable working promise unless its corresponding inputs, fixture data, policy checks, state transitions, negative paths and acceptance tests exist. Human handoff is a complete demo outcome when it provides a real mock case ID, correct queue, required evidence and clear next step.
Sensitive employment actions including promotion, demotion, termination, final pay and disciplinary findings are human workflows. The agent may explain a fictional process or prepare an authorized unsent draft; it must not decide eligibility or outcome. NTE template retrieval and drafting are restricted to an assigned ER persona. General employees can ask for the conduct policy or a confidential review route.

## 5 Entry and lead signup
Proposed route sequence is /demo landing, /demo/register, /demo/session and /demo/session/scenario/{id}. A deep link preserves the intended scenario through registration. Provide a short overview and preview of available categories before requesting details. The CTA is Explore the enterprise demo. Show Fictional company and simulated actions next to the CTA.
Required fields are full name (2 to 100 trimmed characters), work email (syntactically valid, at most 254 characters), company name (2 to 150 characters), and employee-count band (1 to 49, 50 to 199, 200 to 999, 1000 to 3999, 4000 to 9999, 10000 or more). Phone is optional, with a country code and at most 20 characters; blank is valid. Role and areas of interest are optional. Do not reject all personal email domains without a deliberate business decision; show a gentle work-email suggestion instead. Never ask for payroll, government IDs or employee lists.
The form is lead capture, not an employee login or proof of company ownership. Show a concise approved privacy notice explaining demo access, retention and contact use, with a link to the full notice. Optional marketing consent is unchecked and separate. Declining marketing does not block access. Store notice version and choice with a server timestamp. Real collection requires an operator-approved notice and backend; until configured, use fictional test leads only and show Test registration, nothing sent.
Validate both client and server, keep entered values on error, associate inline messages with labels, and focus the first invalid field. Prevent double submit with an idempotency token. Rate-limit by appropriate privacy-preserving signals, provide a honeypot and accessible abuse fallback, and never expose whether an email already exists. A repeated signup can resume the current anonymous demo session without revealing another person's session. Use a random signed session cookie with HttpOnly, Secure and appropriate SameSite settings; never put lead details into a URL.
On success, persist the lead only in the separate lead store if configured, then create an isolated demo session and open the selected scenario. If the lead backend fails, show a retry with preserved inputs; do not claim saved. A CRM delivery failure must not duplicate the lead or silently send it later without recorded delivery state. CRM synchronization is a future adapter, disabled initially. No automated email, SMS or sales notification is supplied by this pack.
Proposed lead fields are lead_id, created_at, full_name, email_normalized, company_name, employee_count_band, phone_optional, role_optional, interest_categories, notice_version, marketing_opt_in, source_campaign_allowlisted, delivery_state and expires_at. Retain for 90 days without engagement as a proposed product setting subject to review. Keep credentials, raw IP addresses, free-text chat and fictional HR data out of the lead schema. Use an operator-configured deletion contact in the approved notice before launch.

## 6 Demo navigation and screen layout
Desktop uses a left rail around 260 pixels, a central employee conversation and a right-side approver and evidence panel. The rail contains search, category dropdown, mode filter, priority-ready status and scenario list. Show Recently viewed and Recommended first without hiding other categories. A selected scenario displays purpose, fictional persona, expected duration, required approvals and whether it is an answer, mock write, draft or handoff.
Keep the existing five-stage progression. For answers, Policy check leads directly to Answer and cited sources without pretending an approval ran. For drafts, the last state is Draft prepared or Sent for mock review. For writes, the approver panel shows outstanding decisions separately from adapter execution. Completed means all required mock acknowledgments exist. Use explicit error and partial-completion states.
The employee chat includes suggested starter prompts plus a genuine input field. Unsupported prompts get a grounded limitation and handoff, not a canned unrelated reply. A request card contains inferred fields, editable values, source citations, balance or amount impact, approvers and Confirm request. The user must explicitly confirm before a mock write. Model inference must never silently submit.
The approver panel has a conspicuous Simulated approver persona label, requester, policy checks, payload version, decision buttons and optional reason. Return for information preserves history and changes state. Reject requires a reason. Approval cannot override a cap exception, missing receipt, missing authority or a stale version. The audit panel shows authorized events, not hidden chain-of-thought, prompts, secrets or personal case details.
A persistent banner says Fictional enterprise demo. All transactions and notifications are simulated. Channel selectors change presentation styling only and must say Channel preview. Show Integration status: Mock on each adapter event. Never imply that clicking Slack sent a Slack message.

## 7 Evidence and generated downloads
A policy answer includes document ID, title, version, effective date, section and a scoped source link. Distinguish Policy source from Employee record as of timestamp. Do not cite a policy to prove a balance, payment or successful provisioning. If there is no permitted source, say the answer is unavailable and offer a handoff.
Employee-facing COEs, policies and NTE templates use ordinary SYDKYK company language. Draft employment documents retain DRAFT labels. The surrounding application preview and separate package disclosure identify fictional content and simulated processing without placing demo-system terminology inside the corporate document. They have no real signatures, seals or official identifiers. Artifact links expire with the session and require server authorization. NTE drafts do not appear in an employee or manager's public download list. The supporting policy PDFs in this pack are fictional source material, not approved company documents.

## 8 Interaction accessibility and mobile
All controls are keyboard reachable, labelled and have visible focus. Use semantic headings, form labels and table headers, a labelled combobox for search/category, and an aria-live polite status area for workflow transitions. Do not announce every token or timer tick. Maintain text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 and nontext contrast of 3 to 1 as implementation targets. Do not encode status in color alone. Respect reduced motion and allow autoplay sequences to pause.
At narrow widths, the selector becomes a labelled drawer and Employee, Approval and Evidence become tabs. Preserve entered draft fields when switching tabs. Test 360-pixel mobile width and 200 percent zoom. No sideways page scroll should be needed for primary actions. Date controls accept ISO dates and visibly show Manila time. Ask for a year or clarify ambiguous dates such as 11/12.

## 9 Mock architecture and future integration boundary
Initial components are a web demo UI, session service, allowlisted persona service, policy retrieval service, structured fixture API, deterministic workflow engine, mock adapters, scoped artifact service and sanitized audit stream. No production credentials, real tenant content or external messaging are necessary. The LLM may interpret and explain; deterministic code validates amounts, schedules, ACLs, approvals and state transitions.
Mock Workday or Sprout Solutions adapter reads employees and balances and writes session-only leave or document requests. Mock ERP records claims and simulated batch status. Mock identity allocates a fixture seat after all approvals. Mock calendar and notifications record events only. Mock ITSM and Workplace create session tickets or bookings. These behaviors are a design contract; the downloaded seed files do not implement them.
Future integrations such as Workday, an ERP, identity provider, Microsoft Graph, Teams, Google Chat, Slack, ITSM or insurer services require customer-specific discovery, licensing, API contract verification, sandbox credentials, field mapping, approved scopes, test evidence and reconciliation. A logo in the deck is not an integration. Enable adapters individually behind a backend allowlist, with a persistent Connected sandbox or Production indicator and a dry-run default. Never switch to live execution because a user asks in chat.
For a real integration, bind approval evidence to a payload hash, use least-privilege credentials, verify webhooks, record provider request IDs, reconcile unknown results and design compensating actions. Do not use a generic fallback that turns a provider timeout into simulated success. Production integration work is outside this document pack.

## 10 RAG and authorization
Only the 17 Markdown knowledge documents enter the curated policy index, subject to access class. The paired PDFs are human-readable copies and should not be indexed again. The included manifest and chunks preserve source IDs, versions, section anchors, hashes, effective dates and permissions. ER-001 is a restricted procedure and template; it is excluded from ordinary employee retrieval. Case-specific ER records are structured restricted data and are never globally embedded.
Filter tenant and permissions before ranking, enforce them on citations and downloads, then recheck before generation and execution. Fail closed on unavailable authorization. Personal data lookups are parameterized server tools scoped to the authenticated fixture persona; a prompt cannot supply an arbitrary employee ID. Cache keys include tenant, persona, grants hash, document versions and session. Prevent stale grants from surviving a persona change.
Use document sections as chunk boundaries with inherited metadata, target 300 to 700 tokens and at most 80 token overlap if splitting long sections. The supplied starter chunk file uses a heading section per chunk and includes metadata verbatim; remeasure token counts for the chosen embedding model. Hybrid keyword/vector retrieval and a reranker are optional implementation choices. Retrieve a small permitted source set, refuse unsupported claims and never use model confidence to bypass a policy decision.

## 11 Measurement and commercial honesty
Track registration_started, registration_succeeded, scenario_opened, question_answered_with_source, draft_confirmed, approval_recorded, mock_adapter_completed, human_handoff_created, reset and demo_completed. Event properties are stable scenario ID, coarse domain, mode, duration bucket, outcome code and anonymous session ID. Lead identity is not a chat analytics property. Do not log free-text prompts, payroll lines, ER content or document evidence.
Report completed mock workflows separately from grounded answers and handoffs. Exclude resets and scripted QA runs. Completion rate denominator is started eligible sessions with an explicit time window. Any illustrative time-saved chart must display assumptions and arithmetic; the demo does not establish real business ROI, autonomous resolution rates or integration performance.

## 12 Delivery sequence and ownership
Product owns scope and labels. HR, Finance, IT and Workplace owners review fictional policy consistency. Engineering owns workflow and authorization. Security owns access tests and incident handling. Sales engineering owns scripts and honest claims. A qualified local HR/legal specialist would validate real policies before customer use; that review is not claimed here.
Stage 1 loads the policy corpus and seed data, establishes tenant isolation and implements the P0 scripts. Stage 2 adds the left selector, registration test mode, mobile behavior, citations and downloadable artifacts. Stage 3 adds P1 scenarios and broadens negative testing. Stage 4 separately evaluates customer sandbox integrations. Do not advertise the entire catalog as executable at the end of Stage 1.
Release requires the engineering acceptance criteria and benchmark cases in this pack to pass in the implemented application. This content pack has been checked for dataset counts, cross-references and file integrity; application behavior remains to be built and tested. No deployment or live application change was performed.
