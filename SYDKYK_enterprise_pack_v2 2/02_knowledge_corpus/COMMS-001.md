# Employee communications learning and resource groups

SYDKYK
Document ID: COMMS-001 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: People Experience | Classification: Internal

## Company announcements
Authorized People Operations or Workplace publishers prepare announcements with an audience, topic, effective dates and source. Broadcasts require publisher approval. Acknowledgment records receipt separately from agreement. A conversational request alone does not authorize sending a message to all employees.

## Learning catalog
Available company courses include SEC101 Security Basics, a one-hour online session with 50 seats; HR101 New Joiner Orientation, a two-hour online session with 30 seats; and LEAD201 Manager Essentials, a three-hour facilitated session with 20 seats. Session times are Philippine time unless otherwise stated.
Enrollment requires employee ID, course and session. A full session uses a waitlist. Paid external learning requires the applicable manager and budget approval before enrollment. A checklist completion does not establish a certification; learning and renewal status must be verified from the authorized record. Employees may view their own history and managers only the completion information permitted for current direct reports.

## Resource groups
New Joiner Circle, Volunteer Network and Learning Exchange provide published descriptions and voluntary participation. Membership is visible only to the member and authorized coordinators. Group participation must not determine compensation, promotion or other employment outcomes. Do not infer protected personal characteristics from a person's interest in a group.

## Employee feedback
Survey invitations state purpose, audience and whether responses are anonymous or confidential. Aggregated reporting requires at least 10 participants per displayed cohort. Suppress smaller groups and prevent filtering that reconstructs an individual response. Feedback must not be used to infer a health condition or an unsupported personal assessment.

## Action inbox and reminders
Employees and managers see tasks assigned to them or covered by a valid delegation. Approve, reject and return-for-information actions require the correct scope and current request version. A reminder does not change status or authorize fulfillment. Escalations go to the responsible service owner when the applicable target is missed.

## Outcome reporting
Service reports distinguish answered questions, completed transactions and human handoffs. Completion requires recorded fulfillment evidence. Exclude withdrawn and cancelled requests from completed-transaction counts. Any estimate of time saved must show its assumptions and must not be presented as a measured result without supporting evidence.
