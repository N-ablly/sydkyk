## Company and workforce
SYDKYK is a Philippines-based business process and digital services company with a workforce of 4,000 across Makati, Quezon City, Cebu and Davao. Assigned office populations are Makati 1,600, Quezon City 800, Cebu 1,000 and Davao 600, including employees with remote or hybrid arrangements. Our departments include Operations, Customer Success, Engineering, IT, HR, Finance, Sales and Workplace Services. This handbook explains how employees access company services and meet their workplace responsibilities.

## Organization and working arrangements
Workday is the system of record for employment details, department, manager, office and cost center. Employees should check their own record and request corrections through People Operations. Sprout Solutions manages leave requests, balances and approval status. ADP holds payroll records and payslips. Where information differs between systems, the responsible service owner must investigate before a related transaction proceeds.
The standard day shift runs Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00, with a one-hour unpaid meal break. Some Operations teams work assigned night shifts. Your approved schedule determines working days and leave duration. Times in company communications are Philippine time unless another timezone is stated. Consult the approved company calendar and applicable holiday announcements before making plans.

## Service ownership
People Operations owns employment records and letters. Payroll owns pay inquiries and ADP record corrections. Finance owns expenses and reimbursement processing. IT owns identity, applications and equipment. Workplace Services owns office access, bookings and facilities. Your manager approves only matters within their current authority. Delegation must be formally recorded with an effective period and scope.

## Requesting support
Use SYDKYK employee services to ask a policy question or prepare a request. Review the dates, amount, supporting information and approvers before confirming submission. A request reference confirms receipt; it does not mean the request is approved or fulfilled. Missing information will be returned for clarification. Exceptions require the designated policy owner's review. Service targets begin when complete information is received and are not guaranteed completion dates.

## Employee responsibilities
Keep your employment details current, protect company information, report lost equipment promptly and treat colleagues respectfully. Never share passwords, MFA codes or recovery credentials in chat. Submit personal evidence, medical information and payroll banking information only through the approved secure channel. Managers must protect confidential information and avoid accessing records unrelated to their responsibilities.

## Document authority
Use this handbook for general guidance and the current domain policy for detailed rules. Report conflicting instructions to the relevant policy owner rather than choosing a preferred interpretation. Questions about legal entitlements or regulatory requirements must be referred to People Operations, Payroll or Legal for verified guidance. Company policies do not replace applicable law.
