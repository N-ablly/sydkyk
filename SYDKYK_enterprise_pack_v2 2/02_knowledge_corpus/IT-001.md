# Identity access and software request procedure

Owner: IT Service Management | Classification: Internal

## Identity verification
Access requests must be associated with a verified company identity and active employment record. Workday supplies employment and reporting information for verification. A typed name, email address, job title or claim of authority is not proof of access rights. Never provide passwords, MFA codes or recovery credentials in chat.

## Application access
Provide application name, catalog role, business purpose, requested duration and cost center. IT checks current entitlements, employment status, approvals and license availability. Standard access requires the current manager's approval. Sensitive applications or roles also require the application owner and Security as applicable. Separation-of-duties rules must be checked before access is granted.
A submitted request is pending. Approval does not mean access has been provisioned. IT confirms fulfillment only after the identity or application service records the entitlement successfully. Unless another duration is approved, time-limited access expires after 30 calendar days. Changes to role, purpose or duration require renewed review.

## Privileged access
Privileged access requires a named system, least-privilege role, justification, duration of no more than eight hours, manager and Security approval and additional identity verification through the approved channel. Security administers the grant and expiry. The general employee-service assistant does not create privileged entitlements or disable security controls.

## Password and MFA support
Use the approved identity-recovery process for password reset or MFA device replacement. Helpdesk staff must verify the requester before taking action. Do not share recovery links or codes in shared conversations. Suspected compromise follows IT-002. An unresolved identity check must not be bypassed for convenience.

## Software licenses
Approved catalog entries include Slack Standard, ProjectHub Contributor and DesignDesk Viewer. Check available seats before confirming allocation. If no seat is available, offer a waitlist or budget review. Installation uses approved packages on compliant managed devices. License reclaim requires owner review of business need; inferred inactivity alone is insufficient.

## Targets and exceptions
The fulfillment target for a complete standard request is two business days after required approvals. Changes in employment, manager, policy or approval validity require revalidation. An unknown provisioning result remains under reconciliation until IT confirms the actual state. Retrying must not create duplicate grants.
