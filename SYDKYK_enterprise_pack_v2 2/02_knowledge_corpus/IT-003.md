# Devices assets and endpoint management

SYDKYK
Document ID: IT-003 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: IT Asset Management | Classification: Internal

## Standard equipment
The standard employee kit includes one managed laptop, charger and headset. A monitor or nonstandard equipment requires manager approval. IT records asset tag, custodian, office, warranty and status in the asset register. Allocation depends on available stock and approved business need.

## Device issue and replacement
Provide asset tag and symptom description. IT diagnoses the issue through an approved support route. Repair or replacement review considers condition, warranty, stock, delivery office and cost. A manager approves nonstandard expenditure and IT approves allocation. Handover records the employee's acknowledgment and the asset state; ticket submission alone does not confirm delivery.

## Asset custody
Report damaged or missing equipment promptly. Do not transfer custody without an IT-recorded handover. Incomplete records are discrepancies requiring review, not evidence of wrongdoing. IT verifies the parties and corrects records where appropriate. Asset-return status does not automatically determine final pay or employment outcome.

## Onboarding and offboarding
IT tasks follow People Operations-approved Workday start dates, employment changes and separation instructions. Identity and equipment may be prepared before the start date, but usable access is confirmed only after activation and required approvals. Revocation uses the authorized effective timestamp. Each system's completion status remains visible until all required actions are confirmed.

## Endpoint compliance
Compliance states are Compliant, Needs update and Unknown. Approved endpoint tools provide the evidence for encryption, updates, security alerts and remediation. A support response must not claim a device is secured or repaired without a verified result. Remediation requiring user involvement is recorded as pending until completed.

## Proactive reviews
IT may flag a warranty nearing expiry or propose a license review based on authorized asset and usage records. The owner confirms business need before replacement or reclamation. Proactive service checks follow the same permissions and review requirements as employee requests and must not be used for covert employee monitoring.
