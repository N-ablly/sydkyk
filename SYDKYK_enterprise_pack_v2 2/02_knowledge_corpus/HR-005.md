# Code of conduct and employee concerns

SYDKYK
Document ID: HR-005 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Employee Relations | Classification: Internal

## Expected behavior
SYDKYK expects employees to treat colleagues respectfully, protect confidential information, report business transactions honestly and use company resources responsibly. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, falsification and threats must be referred for appropriate human review. A concern or allegation is not a finding of misconduct.

## Conflicts gifts and data
Disclose potential conflicts to Employee Relations before taking part in a related procurement, hiring or other business decision. The internal gift-review threshold is PHP 1,000 per item. Cash and cash equivalents always require review. Do not share customer or employee information through an unapproved public service. Suspected data exposure must be reported through the Security process in IT-002.

## Reporting a concern
Employees may use the confidential Employee Relations intake to report a concern. Provide a brief factual summary, approximate date and safe contact preference. Sensitive details and evidence should be supplied only through the restricted case channel. Employee Relations will explain who may review the report; absolute secrecy or a particular outcome cannot be promised.
Concerns involving the employee's manager must be routed outside that manager's review chain. Ordinary concerns have a one-business-day acknowledgment target. Immediate safety concerns require on-site human assistance and appropriate emergency services.

## Fair review
Employee Relations assigns a reviewer, checks conflicts and considers relevant information and responses. Summaries must separate reported facts, disputed points and unknown information. Reviewers must not presume intent or guilt. Only authorized human decision makers may determine findings or employment outcomes.

## Confidentiality and retaliation
Case details are available only to assigned reviewers and other specifically authorized participants. A system-administration role alone does not grant case-content access. Requests to identify a reporter or disclose another person's disciplinary status must be refused unless authorized through the proper process. Retaliation concerns must be referred to Employee Relations.

## Employee acknowledgment
Acknowledgment records that the employee received and acknowledged a document. It does not establish agreement with allegations or waive employee rights. ER-001 governs restricted preparation and review of notices to explain. Employees seeking help with a notice should contact the assigned Employee Relations reviewer.
