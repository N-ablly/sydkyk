# Procurement invoices and finance service requests

SYDKYK
Document ID: FIN-002 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Procurement and Accounts Payable | Classification: Internal

## Purchase requisitions
Provide item category, specification, quantity, unit cost, total amount in PHP, cost center, purpose and required date. Procurement checks approved catalog entries and available inventory first. Requests up to PHP 10,000 require manager and Procurement approval. Requests from PHP 10,000.01 to PHP 100,000 additionally require the budget owner. Requests above PHP 100,000 require those approvals plus the Finance director.

## Vendors and purchase orders
New vendors must complete restricted Procurement and Finance onboarding. Payment details and tax information must be submitted through the approved secure route. Procurement checks conflicts and Finance independently verifies payment instructions. An approved requisition is not itself a purchase order. Only authorized Procurement staff may issue an order or commit company expenditure.

## Invoice matching
Accounts Payable matches each invoice against the purchase order and goods-receipt record. Vendor, quantity, amount, currency and references must agree. A mismatch, missing receipt or possible duplicate requires review. Manager approval does not override a failed match. Posting occurs only after the required checks and approvals are complete.

## Payment status and cash advances
Employees may view their own claim status; Accounts Payable staff may access assigned invoice records. Approved, scheduled, paid and reconciliation required are separate states. A payment is reported as paid only when Finance has a confirmed reference.
Cash advances require Finance approval and subsequent liquidation against eligible receipts. Unliquidated amounts create a review and follow-up task, not an automatic salary deduction. Payroll-related settlement requires a separately approved instruction and Payroll review in ADP.

## Employee finance inquiries
Payroll and payslip questions go to Payroll under HR-003. Expense eligibility follows FIN-001. Tax-document and statutory-calculation requests require a qualified Payroll or tax specialist. Budget availability must be checked against an authorized record rather than inferred from a policy. Amounts use PHP unless another currency is explicitly stated.

## Escalation and targets
Purchase requests and invoice exceptions have a two-business-day acknowledgment target. The assigned owner records missing information, discrepancy and next action. A narrative explanation is not evidence that a ledger posting or payment has occurred.
