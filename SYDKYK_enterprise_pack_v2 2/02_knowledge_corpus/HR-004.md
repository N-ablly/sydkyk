## Preboarding and onboarding
An approved hire record in Workday starts an onboarding checklist tied to employee ID, start date, manager and office. Before Day 1, People Operations checks record completeness and agreement acknowledgment, IT prepares identity and equipment, and Workplace Services prepares office access. Personal identifiers and agreements must be handled through approved secure channels.
Day 1 covers welcome, orientation and access review. Week 1 includes security and job-specific training. Month 1 includes a manager check-in. Days 60 to 90 include scheduled People Operations and manager reviews where applicable. A checklist records operational readiness; it is not an employment-performance assessment. Pending required tasks remain visible and prevent a readiness confirmation.

## Transfers and position changes
Provide proposed department, receiving manager, office, title and effective date. People Operations verifies the receiving position and manager. Current and receiving managers review the change. Compensation changes are reviewed separately by Payroll and Finance.
After approval, People Operations updates Workday. Leave ownership and approver changes are checked in Sprout Solutions, and approved payroll-impacting instructions are referred to Payroll for ADP processing. IT and Workplace Services complete access, equipment and location tasks. Partial completion must be reported by task; the overall move is not complete while required actions remain open.
Promotions and demotions require human review and an authorized decision. A reporting change does not grant access to historic restricted employee-relations records.

## Performance and learning
Employees may view their own review deadlines and assigned tasks. Managers may view only the direct-report review information permitted for the relevant process. Self and peer assessment content remains confidential to authorized participants. Course requests require a course ID and session choice; full sessions use a waitlist. Training completion and certification renewal must be based on verified records.

## Requisitions and employee groups
A headcount requisition includes position, cost center, business reason, proposed headcount and budget owner. People Operations and Finance approve it before recruitment begins. Employee resource groups publish approved descriptions; participation is voluntary and membership access is restricted.

## Separation and clearance
An employee may prepare a resignation request with a proposed last working day for discussion with People Operations. Submission requires explicit employee confirmation and receipt acknowledgment. People Operations determines the required notice, effective dates and applicable obligations with Legal where needed. Termination and retirement decisions remain with authorized human decision makers.
An approved separation checklist coordinates handover, asset return, access revocation, expense reconciliation and clearance. Workday records the approved employment change; Payroll reviews final settlement through ADP. Outstanding equipment does not automatically authorize withholding pay. Former employee requests require a separately verified secure channel.
