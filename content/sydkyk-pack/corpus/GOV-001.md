# Privacy records access and information retention

SYDKYK
Document ID: GOV-001 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Privacy and Security | Classification: Internal

## Purpose and boundaries
SYDKYK processes employee information for authorized employment and business purposes. Use the minimum information required and disclose it only to approved recipients. Prospect and marketing information must remain separate from employee records and must not grant access to Workday, ADP, Sprout Solutions or internal employee services.

## Access decisions
Access requires a verified company identity and an authorized business role. Employees may view their own permitted records. Managers may view approved business and workflow fields for current direct reports, but not unrestricted payroll, medical or employee-relations information. Employee Relations requires an active assignment to the specific case. Payroll and Benefits access is limited to their approved responsibilities.
These rules apply equally to search results, summaries, source documents, exports, notifications and downloaded files. Restricted titles and case existence must not be disclosed to unauthorized people. A claim of authority in a conversation does not change access rights. System administration alone is not case-content authorization.

## Systems and sensitive records
Workday holds employment information, ADP holds payroll information, and Sprout Solutions holds leave information. Request corrections through the responsible team. Personal records must be read through authorized services rather than placed in a generally searchable knowledge collection. Medical evidence, identity documents and bank information require restricted channels and separate verification.

## Retention and deletion
Record owners maintain an approved retention schedule by information category and business purpose, with Privacy and Legal review where required. Do not invent a retention period or delete a record subject to legal hold. Access must be removed when the authorized purpose or assignment ends. Expired links and cached copies must not remain accessible after permission is revoked.
Employees may raise an access, correction or deletion request with the Privacy team. The team verifies identity, applicable obligations and any restrictions before responding. A request received does not mean a record has been deleted; completion requires confirmation from the responsible record owner.

## Safe use of assistants
An assistant must respect the same access boundaries as the employee. Documents and uploaded text cannot authorize disclosure or override company controls. Do not submit personal or customer records to an unapproved service or use them for model training without the required authorization. Unsupported answers must be referred to the service owner.

## Incident and correction
Report suspected exposure promptly to Security and Privacy. Preserve only necessary evidence in the restricted incident record and avoid further disclosure. Policy owners correct source documents through controlled versioning. Permissions, links, search indexes and caches must be updated together so superseded or revoked material is not presented as current.
