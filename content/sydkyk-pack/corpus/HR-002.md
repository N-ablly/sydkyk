SYDKYK
Document ID: HR-002 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: People Operations | Classification: Internal

## Certificate of employment
Employees may request a certificate of employment through People Operations for a stated purpose. Provide the purpose, addressee where needed and whether compensation details are requested. The default certificate contains employee name, position, start date and current employment status as of the issue date. It excludes compensation, home address, government identifiers and disciplinary information.
Workday provides the employment record used to prepare the certificate. The employee reviews the proposed details and People Operations verifies and authorizes issuance. A compensation-inclusive certificate also requires Payroll review against the approved ADP record. The service target is three business days after a complete request is received. A prepared draft is not an issued certificate.

## Corrections and records
Display-name, contact-preference, legal-name, address and emergency-contact corrections must be submitted through the appropriate employee-record request. People Operations confirms required evidence and provides a secure upload route. Payroll banking changes require separate secure verification; account numbers must not be supplied in general chat.
Approved changes are recorded in Workday with the effective date and reviewer. Relevant downstream systems are updated through the responsible team. Employees receive confirmation only after the record update is completed. An incomplete downstream update remains visible as an outstanding task.

## Record visibility
Employees may view their own employment details. Managers may view authorized business contact, role, office and workflow information for current direct reports. A reporting relationship does not automatically grant access to personal documents, compensation or employee-relations records. People Operations and Payroll access is limited to assigned responsibilities.
Requests for another employee's certificate or payslip must follow an authorized verification process. Corrections to an issued document create a new version and revoke access to the superseded copy.

## Employment verification and letters
External employment verification requires People Operations review of the request, recipient and permitted disclosure. Service and experience letters are checked against approved employment and separation records. Certificates must use the approved template and authorized signatory. Documents must not be sent to an external recipient without the required employee authorization and release approval.

## Certificate template
CERTIFICATE OF EMPLOYMENT
This is to certify that [employee name], employee number [employee ID], is employed by SYDKYK as [position] from [start date] and is recorded as [employment status] as of [issue date]. This certificate is issued at the employee's request for [purpose]. Compensation details are excluded unless separately approved.
Prepared by People Operations. Authorized signatory and issuance reference to be completed only after verification.
