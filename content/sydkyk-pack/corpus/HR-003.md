# Payroll benefits and compensation service guide

SYDKYK
Document ID: HR-003 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Payroll and Benefits | Classification: Internal

## Payroll inquiries
ADP is SYDKYK's payroll system. Payroll is scheduled twice monthly, on the 15th and last calendar day, with a prior company working-day fallback. Attendance cutoffs are the 10th and 25th at 18:00 Philippine time. Payroll reviews approved changes and any closed-period adjustments before they enter a later pay run.
Employees may request their own payslip and an explanation of its recorded line items. An explanation must distinguish posted amounts from estimates or pending corrections. Statutory deduction, tax, contribution, premium and 13th-month pay questions must be referred to Payroll for verified calculations. Do not treat a general policy answer as a payroll calculation.

## Discrepancy handling
Identify the pay period and disputed item when raising an inquiry. Payroll may compare the employee's ADP payslip with authorized attendance and leave information, including Sprout Solutions leave records. The acknowledgment target is two business days. Manager approval of an attendance correction does not by itself authorize an ADP adjustment or guarantee a refund.
Payroll confirms the investigation outcome, adjustment period and record reference where applicable. No adjustment is complete until the payroll record has been updated and checked. Personal banking information and supporting evidence must use Payroll's secure verification process.

## Benefits
The company health plan provides an annual medical limit of PHP 150,000 per eligible regular employee, subject to the applicable plan terms and Benefits confirmation. Dependent enrollment requires eligibility review and either a qualifying event or an enrollment window. The annual enrollment window is November 1 to 30, 2026.
Provide the relationship category and event date. Submit identity or medical evidence only through the restricted Benefits channel. Coverage is confirmed only after Benefits has validated eligibility and received the insurer's acknowledgment. A request received is not confirmation of coverage.

## Compensation and one time payments
Managers may propose merit adjustments, spot awards, referral payouts or relocation allowances for current direct reports. Each proposal requires employee ID, purpose, amount, currency, cost center and effective date. People Operations and Finance review the proposal; Payroll records the approved instruction in ADP.
Access to compensation is restricted. Managers may not use the general employee-service channel to obtain salary history or another employee's payslip. Promotions, demotions and adverse employment actions require authorized human decisions and cannot be decided by an assistant.

## Sensitive answers
Do not disclose another person's pay, medical information or private benefit evidence. Requests outside the employee's authorized scope must be referred to the appropriate service owner. Employees may ask for clarification or correction without being promised a particular financial outcome.
