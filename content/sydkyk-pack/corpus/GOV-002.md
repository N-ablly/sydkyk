# Approval delegation and service operating model

Document ID: GOV-002 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Shared Services Governance | Classification: Internal

## Approval authority
Approval authority comes from an effective company record. Managers approve eligible requests for current direct reports within their limits. A requester cannot approve their own request. Finance, application-owner, Security, People Operations and Procurement approvals are additional controls where required. Seniority alone does not override them.
Workday is the reference for reporting relationships. Leave approvals and balances are managed in Sprout Solutions. Payroll instructions require authorized Payroll review before ADP processing. A change in manager or employment status requires pending approvals to be revalidated.

## Delegation
A delegation must identify delegator, delegate, workflow types, effective start and end time and any amount limit. Further delegation is prohibited unless explicitly permitted. Requests outside the recorded scope must be declined or rerouted. The original authority and delegation validity must be checked when the delegate acts.

## Workflow states
Draft means editable and not submitted. Submitted means the requester confirmed the exact information. Pending approval means one or more required decisions remain. Approved means all required decisions are recorded for the current request version. Processing means fulfillment has started. Completed means all required actions have confirmed results.
Rejected, cancelled, failed, partially completed and reconciliation required are distinct outcomes. Changing an amount, role, date or recipient requires a new version and renewed relevant approvals. Retrying a request must not duplicate a booking, expense or leave reservation.

## Service clocks
Business service hours are 09:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding applicable closures. P1 Security acknowledgment is measured continuously. Company communications show Philippine time unless otherwise stated. A clock may pause for missing requester information only with a recorded reason and applicable rule. An acknowledgment target must not be presented as a resolution guarantee.

## Audit and handoff
Record actor, request reference, version, event, timestamp, applicable policy, decision and relevant system reference. Sensitive evidence belongs in restricted records, not a shared activity panel. A handoff provides necessary context, permitted evidence references, outstanding decision and next owner.

## Exceptions
Conflicting policy, missing authority, stale balance or unknown processing status stops fulfillment until reviewed. Explain what remains outstanding and route it to the responsible owner. An assistant's confidence or a user's request for urgency cannot replace an approval or confirmed system result.
