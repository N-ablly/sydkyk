# Expenses reimbursements and business travel

Document ID: FIN-001 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Finance Operations | Classification: Internal

## Expense rules
Expense claims must include business purpose, transaction date, amount, currency, category, cost center and an itemized receipt. Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Personal expenses are ineligible. Alcohol and entertainment require explicit Finance exception review. Foreign-currency claims require a documented rate source and Finance validation.
Domestic travel caps are PHP 800 per person per day for meals, PHP 4,000 per hotel night and PHP 1,500 per day for local transport. These are internal spending limits, not tax thresholds. Amounts above a cap require Finance exception review. Claims must not be silently reduced or treated as a misconduct finding solely because they exceed a limit.

## Approval path
Claims up to PHP 5,000 require the employee's manager and Finance validation. Claims above PHP 5,000 also require the cost-center budget owner. Self-approval is prohibited. Finance checks receipt details, date, category, eligibility and possible duplicates. A matching receipt, date and amount indicates a possible duplicate for review, not proof of wrongdoing.

## Submission and payout
Review all extracted receipt fields before confirming submission. Approved claims are scheduled for the next eligible weekly payment batch, subject to Finance validation and processing cutoffs. Received, approved, scheduled and paid are distinct statuses. A paid status requires a confirmed payment reference; approval alone does not establish that funds were transferred.
Payment and bank-account information must use approved secure Finance channels. Employee payroll enquiries and any payroll-related adjustment are handled by Payroll through ADP. Do not assume an expense reimbursement has been included in ADP unless Payroll confirms that route for the specific claim.
Rejected claims include a reason and a resubmission route. If processing status is unknown, Finance reconciles the existing request before another payment attempt is authorized. Repeated submission must not create a second reimbursement.

## Worked examples
A PHP 1,250 local-transport expense for one day is within the PHP 1,500 cap and requires manager and Finance review. A PHP 6,500 claim additionally requires the budget owner. A second claim using the same receipt is held for duplicate review. A PHP 950 meal expense for one person for one day requires a cap exception even if the manager approves it.

## Travel planning
Travel authorization precedes booking and includes itinerary, purpose, estimated budget and cost center. The manager confirms the business need and applicable budget approval. Finance reviews cash advances and reconciles them against receipts. Only authorized staff may commit to bookings, orders or payments.
