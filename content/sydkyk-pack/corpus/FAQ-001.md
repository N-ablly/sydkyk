## Vacation balance
How do I check my vacation balance and request leave?
View your current balance and submit the proposed dates in Sprout Solutions. The request shows chargeable days and the approval owner before submission. Submitted leave is pending until your authorized manager approves it. HR-001 explains calculation, reservations and cancellation.

## Certificate request
How do I get a certificate of employment for a visa?
Send People Operations the purpose, addressee if needed and whether compensation is requested. Employment details are checked against Workday. Payroll verifies any approved compensation details against ADP. A prepared draft is not an issued certificate. See HR-002.

## Expense reimbursement
How do I claim a client-visit transport expense?
Provide date, amount, purpose, cost center and itemized receipt. FIN-001 sets the local-transport cap at PHP 1,500 per day. Your manager and Finance review the claim; claims above PHP 5,000 also need the budget owner. Approved and paid are different statuses.

## Access and devices
How do I request ProjectHub access?
Select the catalog role and provide purpose and duration. IT checks license availability and the required manager and application-owner approvals. Access is available only after IT confirms provisioning. See IT-001.
What should I do if my laptop is missing?
Report the asset tag and approximate time to IT and Security promptly. Do not send passwords or recovery codes. Security determines and confirms containment actions. See IT-002 and IT-003.

## Pay and sensitive requests
Where can I see my payslip?
Use your authorized ADP access or request help from Payroll. Payroll can explain your own recorded line items and investigate a discrepancy. An approved attendance correction does not automatically guarantee a refund. See HR-003.
Can I obtain another employee's salary or disciplinary details?
General employee services cannot disclose that information. Contact the responsible team if you have a specific authorized business requirement.

## Legal questions and unavailable information
Who can confirm statutory deductions, protected leave or notice requirements?
People Operations, Payroll or Legal must provide verified guidance for your circumstances. A company policy is not a substitute for applicable law.
If a source is unavailable or current policies conflict, the request will be referred to the policy owner. Completion must not be promised while required information or approval is missing.
