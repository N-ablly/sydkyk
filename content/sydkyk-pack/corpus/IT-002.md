# IT incident security and network support

Owner: Security Operations | Classification: Internal

## Incident intake
Provide the affected service or asset, office, start time, business impact and a short symptom summary. Do not include credentials, access tokens or unrelated personal data. IT assigns a ticket reference, owner and next update time. Related widespread reports may be linked to a parent incident.

## Severity and service targets
P1 covers suspected active security compromise or a broad critical-service outage, with a 15-minute acknowledgment target around the clock. P2 covers an employee blocked from critical work, with a one-business-hour acknowledgment target. P3 covers degraded service, with a four-business-hour target. P4 covers routine requests, with a one-business-day target. These are acknowledgment targets, not promised resolution times. Human support confirms the severity and response.

## Phishing and lost devices
Stop interacting with a suspected phishing message and report it through the approved secure channel. Do not open suspicious attachments or provide credentials for investigation. For a lost device, provide its asset tag if known, approximate time of loss and whether company information may be affected. Security determines containment, device-lock or other authorized actions and confirms completion through the incident record.

## VPN and connectivity
Check the service-status notice before troubleshooting. Establish whether the issue affects one device or an office. Safe checks include confirming internet connectivity, restarting the approved client and checking device time. Do not disable endpoint protection, bypass certificate warnings or install unapproved networking tools. Repeated failures should be escalated with sanitized diagnostics.

## Email and collaboration
Mailbox and calendar issues require a service ticket. Distribution-list membership changes need the list owner's approval. External members require Security review. Shared-mailbox access requires manager and mailbox-owner approval. Membership in a chat channel does not establish permission to underlying files or employee information.

## Handoff and notifications
A handoff contains the permitted summary, affected service, ticket ID, urgency, checks completed and evidence references. It excludes credentials and unrelated HR history. Security details are shared only with authorized recipients. Employees receive a status update only when supported by the incident record; a request received is not confirmation that containment has occurred.
