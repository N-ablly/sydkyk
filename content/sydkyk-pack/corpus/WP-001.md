# Workplace facilities visitors and employee safety

SYDKYK
Document ID: WP-001 | Version: 1.1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Review: 2027-10-01
Owner: Workplace Services | Classification: Internal

## Office and desk access
SYDKYK offices are in Makati, Quezon City, Cebu and Davao. Desk requests require office, date and time window. Workplace Services checks capacity before confirming a resource and prevents overlapping active reservations. A pending request is not a confirmed booking. Badge issuance and changes require verified identity and the applicable Workplace or Security approval.

## Rooms visitors and parking
Room bookings include location, start and end time and required capacity. Accessibility information volunteered by an employee is handled confidentially. A visitor request includes host, visitor company, visit window and purpose. The host approves the visit; Security authorizes restricted areas. Parking requests follow the assigned office's capacity and waitlist rules.

## Facilities incidents
Report office, general area, issue category and urgency. Electrical hazards, fire, threats and medical emergencies require immediate on-site human assistance and appropriate emergency services. A service request is not confirmation that emergency help has been dispatched. Routine lighting, cleaning and air-conditioning requests have a one-business-day acknowledgment target.

## Flexible work and accessibility
Employment-related remote-work, relocation and office-transfer approvals follow HR-001 and HR-004. Workplace Services coordinates approved equipment and space tasks after People Operations confirmation. Workday holds the approved office assignment. Leave approvals continue through Sprout Solutions. Accessibility requests use a confidential review route and do not require a diagnosis in general chat.

## Closure notices
Authorized Workplace announcements specify location, effective dates and source. Consult the current company calendar and official applicable holiday announcements. An omitted calendar entry should be referred for clarification rather than treated as a legal determination that a date is a working day.

## Data handling
Employees may see their own bookings, hosts their own visitor requests, and Workplace staff their assigned service records. Avoid exposing another booker's identity when explaining a capacity conflict. Visitor and accessibility information follows approved purpose, access and retention rules.
