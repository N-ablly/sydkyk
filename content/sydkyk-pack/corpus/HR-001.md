# Leave attendance and work arrangements

## Company leave rules
Regular full-time employees receive an annual company vacation allocation of 15 working days, sick allocation of 10 working days and emergency allocation of 3 working days. These company benefits do not replace other applicable statutory or protected leave. People Operations confirms eligibility for other employment types and leave categories.
Submit vacation requests in Sprout Solutions at least five scheduled working days before the proposed start date. Shorter notice requires manager and People Operations exception review. A vacation reason is optional. Do not include a diagnosis in ordinary chat. If medical evidence is required, People Operations will provide a restricted submission route.

## Balance and duration
Sprout Solutions is the source for current leave balances and request status. Available leave equals allocation plus approved adjustments minus taken days, approved future days and pending reserved days. A submitted request reserves the requested days. Approval converts that reservation into approved future leave without a second deduction. Rejection or withdrawal releases the reservation. Cancellation of approved leave requires manager approval before the balance is restored.
Duration follows the employee's approved schedule and applicable company calendar. A full scheduled day is 1.0 day and a half day is 0.5 day. Overlapping active leave requests are not permitted. Requests exceeding available paid leave must be discussed with People Operations; they are not automatically converted to unpaid leave.

## Approvals and status
Review leave type, dates, chargeable days, balance impact and manager before submission. The assigned manager approves or rejects the request in Sprout Solutions. The approval target is two business days. A pending request is not approved leave. Managers cannot approve their own requests; approval moves to their manager or another authorized reviewer.
Approved leave must be recorded successfully in Sprout Solutions before related calendar updates are confirmed. Changes to dates or leave type require fresh review. If a reporting relationship or policy changes while a request is pending, the appropriate approver and rules must be checked again.

## Attendance and schedules
A missing punch is an attendance exception to investigate, not proof of absence. Provide the date, expected shift, claimed actual hours and reason. Manager approval of a correction creates a Payroll review where the pay period is closed. Payroll confirms any ADP adjustment; attendance approval alone does not promise a pay reversal.
Overtime requires manager authorization and validated time records. People Operations and Payroll determine applicable treatment. Shift changes, remote-work arrangements and location changes require a proposed effective date and manager and People Operations approval, with IT or Workplace tasks where needed.

## Examples
An employee with 12.0 available vacation days requesting three scheduled days would have 9.0 available while the request is reserved. An employee with 1.0 day available cannot submit the same three-day request as fully paid vacation. People Operations can discuss alternatives without changing the requested leave category automatically.
