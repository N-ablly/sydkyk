# Package disclosure and company system conventions

The enclosed corporate policies, employee documents and records are authored fictional supporting content for an enterprise demonstration. They are not adopted SYDKYK policies, verified statements about a real employer, legal advice, issued employment certificates or valid disciplinary notices. Corporate document bodies are intentionally written in normal company language for realistic knowledge retrieval. Keep this disclosure with the package when sharing it.

## User approved naming
The company name is SYDKYK, spelled S-Y-D-K-Y-K. Workday is the named employee-record and HRIS system. ADP is the named payroll system. Sprout Solutions is the named leave-management system. These assignments are narrative and integration-design choices, not evidence of configured software or verified vendor features.

## Integration boundary
No Workday, ADP or Sprout Solutions account, credentials, connector or transaction is supplied or activated. All executable demo outcomes remain mock operations. Leave balances and requests map to the Sprout Solutions mock adapter; employment records and lifecycle changes map to the Workday mock adapter; payslip explanations and approved payroll-adjustment tasks map to the ADP mock adapter. Finance and identity adapters remain generic mock interfaces. Exact vendor APIs, workflow ownership, access scopes and payroll coverage must be validated during customer discovery.
